Our Story

A Family Legacy in Every Scoop

Divino Gelato Cafe was born in 2003 from a passion for authentic Italian gelato. Founded by Lori Lester and her family, Divino has grown from a single location into a beloved three-location institution — serving over 220 handcrafted flavors made fresh every day.

Unlike mass-produced ice cream, our gelato is made in small batches with high-quality ingredients — less air, less fat, more flavor. Every bite is the result of twenty years of craft and love for the people of Wisconsin.
